# LearnPro LMS — Phase 1 Launch

Phase 1 is intentionally focused on MCQs and study materials. Video classes remain supported by the underlying course model and can be added later without rebuilding existing courses or requiring existing students to repurchase.

## Launch content
- Courses and public course previews
- Student registration/login and OTP verification
- Razorpay payment verification and enrollment
- Study-material uploads with Demo/Premium access
- MCQ management, CSV/PDF/OCR import and review
- Demo and Full Tests with results and explanations
- Student progress and dashboard
- Admin student management, payments and analytics
- Notifications
- Two active devices per student

## Later
- Add chapters/classes and secure video playback as videos become available.
